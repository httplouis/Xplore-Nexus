## EDITING WORKSHEETS AND ENTERING DATA

In Excel application, all the data is entered in a worksheet. In this video, you will learn to identify the different components of a worksheet and see how to enter, edit, and clear the data in the cells.

In Microsoft Excel, a worksheet is a single spreadsheet page. Within a workbook, it is a primary working area where you can enter and manipulate data, perform calculations, and organize information. You can have multiple worksheets within a single workbook.

You can also click on the left corner of the worksheet to select the entire active worksheet. And click on any cell to unselect the entire active worksheet.

A worksheet consists of a grid made up of rows and columns:
- Rows are horizontal lines that run from left to right. Each row is typically represented by a number. If you click on row 1, then you will select the entire first row.
- Columns are vertical lines in the spreadsheet that run from top to bottom represented by a letter. If you click on column A, then you will select the entire first column.

Cells are the intersection points of the rows and columns where you can enter and store the data in the Excel worksheet that contains text, numbers, formulas, or various data types to analyze and present your data effectively. Each cell is identified by a unique combination of column letter and a row number. For example, if we see for the selected cell, go to the name box and here you will find the cell name as A1 where A is your column letter and 1 is your row number.

In Excel, data is entered into cells. To select a cell, simply go to it and click on it. Then you can start typing your data. For example, here I want to mention my company name as "Homotic". Then to move to the next cell, there are two ways:
1. You can use the arrow keys on your keyboard. For example, if I want to move to the right, then I can use the right arrow key.
2. Or the second way is simply go and click on the desired cell.

You can also use the formula bar for some complex entries. For example, let's say here you want to enter "Zebrasky". Then you can go to the formula bar, click on it to make it active, and then you can start typing your text. Then you can hit Tab in the keyboard to move to the next cell in the row.

Now, if you entered any incorrect information in the cell, then you can easily edit the data. There are different ways to do this:

The first way is to go to the cell, double-click on it, or even you can use the F2 key as the keyboard shortcut to make the changes in the cell. Then you can do the amendments. For example, here I want to mention "Company". When I press Enter, it submits this change and selects the next cell down in the column.

Or the second way of editing the data in cell is you can go select the cell, then go to the formula bar, click on it to make it active, and then you can make the changes. For example I want to remove "sky" and only want to keep "zebra", and when I again press Enter, it selects the next cell down in the column.

Now, what if you want to delete the entire data within the cell? How you can do it? Let's move to our second worksheet and do them in two different scenarios.

In my first scenario, I want to delete the content of my first column. So I'm first of all going to select the range of the cells, then I will go to the Home tab, then to the Editing section, then to the Clear option and click on the dropdown menu. From the dropdown menu, I will select Clear Contents and you will see that the data has been deleted from the selected cell.

Or the other method is go and select the range of cells first and then use the keyboard shortcut as Delete. Or if you are using Mac then you can use Function + Delete, and then you will notice that the data has been deleted from the selected range of cells.
