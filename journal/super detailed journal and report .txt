SUMMARY OF DISCUSSION:

• For the TNA assessment, 2 levels lang muna yung magiging classification which is Basic and Advanced.

• The respondent’s overall level will be determined based sa average score nila from all assessment questions.

• Possible na advanced yung respondent sa isang specific category pero basic sa ibang categories, so kailangan makita both yung overall classification and detailed per-category results sa summary/report.

• Example: pwedeng advanced yung respondent sa “Worksheet and Workbook Management” pero hindi sa “Working with Graphic Objects,” so dapat reflected pareho sa results.

• Napag-usapan din na kahit may strong performance sa isang category, overall classification pa rin will depend sa combined average or metrics ng buong assessment.

• Segmentation can be based on classifications such as technical vs non-technical respondents.

• Required respondent information may include rank, age bracket, position classification, and other details needed for reporting and analysis.

• Possible sa system na mag-add nalang ng fields, placeholders, or buttons where respondents can input these details, depending sa magiging implementation and database structure.

• Custom inputs are allowed for respondent information and segmentation fields.

• Segmentation will happen within the survey/assessment flow itself.

• Collected data and segmentation results will be used for analytics, reporting, and identifying how many respondents belong under each level/category.

• Possible rin na gamitin yung results for identifying training needs and recommended learning paths per respondent.

• Suggestion ni Sir Rolan na possible makapagbigay rin ng valuable insights yung marketing team regarding the structure of the survey questions, flow, and overall assessment approach.
